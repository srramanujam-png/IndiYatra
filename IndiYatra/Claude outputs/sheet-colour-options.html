Floating action panel and explanation sheet: colour options

Every colour comes from the IndiYatra palette or from tints already used in the app (saffron, heritage blue, cultural green, teal browse colour, surface tint). All four options share the same separation cues so the sheet reads as its own layer: a rounded top with a visible edge, an upward shadow, a darker drag handle and a circled close button. Use the switch to see them with the page dimmed behind the sheet.

1. Neutral grey

Calm and safe. Sheet uses the site's sub-divider grey; the panel is white with a heritage-blue outline.
